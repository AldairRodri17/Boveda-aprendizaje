# Inglés A1 - presente simple

Parte de: [[49-Idiomas/02 Inglés A1/_Índice Inglés A1|Inglés A1]]

> [!info] Cómo avanzar
> `progreso`: **pendiente** → **viendo** → **visto** → **dominado**.

## Contenido

### Qué es
El **presente simple** se usa para **hábitos, rutinas, hechos generales y horarios**. No es para lo que pasa "en este momento" (eso es [[Inglés A2 - presente continuo]]).

### Cómo se forma
| | I / you / we / they | he / she / it |
|---|---|---|
| Afirmativa | I **work** | she **works** |
| Negativa | I **don't** work | she **doesn't** work |
| Pregunta | **Do** you work? | **Does** she work? |
| Respuesta corta | Yes, I do. / No, I don't. | Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't. |

**La regla de la -s (tercera persona):**
- Normal: work → work**s**, play → play**s**
- Termina en -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o: watch → watch**es**, go → go**es**, do → do**es**
- Consonante + y: study → stud**ies**
- Irregular: have → **has**

**Importante:** con *does/doesn't* el verbo regresa a su forma base: She doesn't work**s** ✗ → She doesn't **work** ✓.

**Adverbios de frecuencia** (van antes del verbo principal, pero después de *to be*): always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never.
- I **usually** start at 9. / She is **always** late.

### Ejemplos
- I work from home on Mondays.
- The deploy runs every night at 2 a.m.
- My team doesn't use Java.
- Does your company offer remote work?
- Water boils at 100 °C.

### Errores comunes de hispanohablantes
- Olvidar la -s: ~~He work in a bank.~~ → He work**s**.
- Doble marca: ~~Does he works?~~ → Does he **work**?
- Usar presente simple para ahora: ~~I eat now.~~ → I'**m eating** now.
- Negar sin auxiliar: ~~I no like coffee.~~ → I **don't** like coffee.

## Autoevaluación

> [!question]- Conjuga: "She (study) English and (watch) series every night."
> She **studies** English and **watches** series every night.

> [!question]- Corrige: "My boss don't speak Spanish."
> My boss **doesn't** speak Spanish.

> [!question]- Haz la pregunta: "Ellos trabajan los sábados" → ¿?
> **Do** they work on Saturdays?

> [!question]- ¿Dónde va "usually" en "I am late"?
> I am **usually** late (después de *to be*).
