# Inglés A1 - there is y there are

Parte de: [[49-Idiomas/02 Inglés A1/_Índice Inglés A1|Inglés A1]]

> [!info] Cómo avanzar
> `progreso`: **pendiente** → **viendo** → **visto** → **dominado**.

## Contenido

### Qué es
**There is / there are** equivale a **"hay"** en español: sirve para decir que algo existe o está en un lugar. A diferencia del español, cambia según si lo que sigue es singular o plural.

### Cómo se forma
| | Singular / incontable | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| Afirmativa | There **is** (There's) a problem. | There **are** two problems. |
| Negativa | There **isn't** any milk. | There **aren't** any tests. |
| Pregunta | **Is there** a bathroom? | **Are there** any questions? |
| Respuesta corta | Yes, there is. / No, there isn't. | Yes, there are. / No, there aren't. |
| Pasado | There **was** a bug. | There **were** many users. |

**Some y any:**
- **some** en afirmativas: There are **some** errors in the log.
- **any** en negativas y preguntas: There aren't **any** errors. Are there **any** errors?

**How many / How much:**
- How many **people** are there? (contable)
- How much **memory** is there? (incontable)

### Ejemplos
- There's a meeting room on the second floor.
- There are three pull requests waiting for review.
- Is there a good café near the office?
- There weren't any issues during the release.

### Errores comunes de hispanohablantes
- Traducir "hay" con *have*: ~~Have a problem.~~ → **There is** a problem.
- ~~There is many people.~~ → There **are** many people.
- Confundir *there* (ahí, hay), *their* (su de ellos) y *they're* (they are). Suenan igual.
- Usar *it has*: ~~In my city it has a big park.~~ → In my city **there is** a big park.

## Autoevaluación

> [!question]- Completa: "___ a lot of traffic today and ___ two accidents on the highway."
> **There is** a lot of traffic and **there are** two accidents.

> [!question]- Haz la pregunta: "¿Hay alguna pregunta?"
> **Are there any** questions?

> [!question]- Corrige: "In my house have three bedrooms."
> In my house **there are** three bedrooms.
