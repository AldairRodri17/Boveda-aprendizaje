# Inglés B2 - conectores para hablar y escribir

Parte de: [[49-Idiomas/05 Inglés B2/_Índice Inglés B2|Inglés B2]]

> [!info] Cómo avanzar
> `progreso`: **pendiente** → **viendo** → **visto** → **dominado**.

## Contenido

### Qué es
Los **conectores** (*linking words*) unen ideas y muestran la relación entre ellas: contraste, causa, consecuencia, adición. Son la diferencia más visible entre un B1 ("and… and… but…") y un B2.

### Cómo funciona
| Función | Informal / hablado | Formal / escrito |
|---|---|---|
| **Agregar** | and, also, plus, too | in addition, furthermore, moreover |
| **Contrastar** | but, though, still | however, nevertheless, on the other hand, whereas |
| **Concesión** | even though, although | despite / in spite of + sustantivo o -ing |
| **Causa** | because, since, as | due to, owing to + sustantivo |
| **Consecuencia** | so, that's why | therefore, as a result, consequently, thus |
| **Ejemplo** | like, for example | for instance, such as, e.g. |
| **Aclarar** | I mean, basically | in other words, that is (i.e.) |
| **Secuencia** | first, then, after that, finally | firstly, subsequently, lastly |
| **Concluir** | so, all in all | in conclusion, to sum up, overall |

**Gramática que hay que cuidar:**
- **although / even though** + oración: *Although it's expensive, it's worth it.*
- **despite / in spite of** + sustantivo o -ing: *Despite **the cost**, …* / *Despite **being** expensive, …* (~~despite it is~~ ✗)
- **however** va al inicio con coma o entre comas: *It's fast. **However**, it's expensive.* (no une dos oraciones con coma: ~~It's fast, however it's expensive~~ en escritura formal → usa punto o punto y coma).
- **because** + oración / **because of** + sustantivo: *because it failed* / *because of the failure*.

### Ejemplos
- *The migration took longer than expected **due to** data inconsistencies. **As a result**, we postponed the release.*
- ***Although** serverless is cheap at low traffic, it can get expensive at scale. **Therefore**, we chose containers.*
- *We need better monitoring, **such as** alerts on latency and error rates.*

### Errores comunes de hispanohablantes
- ~~Despite of~~ → **Despite** / **In spite of**.
- Abusar de *but* y *so*: varía.
- Usar conectores formales en Slack (suena raro): *Furthermore, I'll be late* → *Also, I'll be late*.
- ~~Although… but…~~ (doble contraste) → elige uno.

## Autoevaluación

> [!question]- Corrige: "Despite it was raining, we went out."
> **Although** it was raining, … / **Despite the rain**, …

> [!question]- Une con un conector de consecuencia formal: "The tests failed. We canceled the deploy."
> The tests failed. **Therefore** / **As a result**, we canceled the deploy.

> [!question]- ¿Because o because of?: "The outage happened ___ a bad configuration."
> **because of** (seguido de sustantivo).
