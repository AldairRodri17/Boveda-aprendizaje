# Inglés B2 - debatir y argumentar

Parte de: [[49-Idiomas/05 Inglés B2/_Índice Inglés B2|Inglés B2]]

## Contenido

### Qué es
En B2 ya no basta con opinar: necesitas **defender una postura con argumentos, responder objeciones y llegar a acuerdos**. Es lo que pasa en revisiones de arquitectura, discusiones de diseño y negociaciones.

### Cómo funciona: estructura de un argumento (PREP)
1. **P**oint: tu postura. *I think we should use PostgreSQL.*
2. **R**eason: la razón principal. *The main reason is cost…*
3. **E**xample: evidencia. *For instance, our current license costs…*
4. **P**oint again: cierra. *So overall, PostgreSQL is the better option for us.*

### Frases por función
| Función | Frases |
|---|---|
| Presentar postura | I'd argue that… · My view is that… · I'm convinced that… |
| Matizar | To some extent… · In most cases… · It depends on… |
| Evidencia | The data shows that… · Based on my experience… · Studies suggest… |
| Anticipar objeciones | Some people might say… but… · I know it's more expensive; however… |
| Desacuerdo educado | I see where you're coming from, but… · I'm not entirely convinced. · Have we considered…? |
| Concesión | That's a fair point. · I'll give you that. · You have a point there. |
| Pedir aclaración | What exactly do you mean by…? · Could you elaborate on that? |
| Interrumpir con cortesía | Sorry to interrupt, but… · Can I just add something? |
| Buscar acuerdo | Can we agree that…? · What if we…? · Maybe we could meet halfway. |
| Resumir | So, to sum up, … · Let me make sure I understand… |

### Ejemplo
> — *I think we should move to microservices.*
> — *I see where you're coming from, but I'm not entirely convinced. Our team is only five people. **Some might say** microservices scale better, **however**, they also add operational complexity. **For instance**, we'd need service discovery, distributed tracing… **What if we** start with a modular monolith and extract services only when we need to?*
> — *That's a fair point. Can we agree to revisit it in six months?*

### Errores comunes de hispanohablantes
- Sonar agresivo al traducir directo ("No, you are wrong"). Suaviza: *I'm not sure that's right.*
- No conceder nada: en inglés profesional se espera reconocer el punto del otro.
- ~~I am agree / disagree~~ → I **agree** / **disagree**.
