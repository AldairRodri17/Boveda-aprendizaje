# Inglés B2 - estilo indirecto (reported speech)

Parte de: [[49-Idiomas/05 Inglés B2/_Índice Inglés B2|Inglés B2]]

> [!info] Cómo avanzar
> `progreso`: **pendiente** → **viendo** → **visto** → **dominado**.

## Contenido

### Qué es
El **estilo indirecto** sirve para **contar lo que alguien dijo** sin citarlo textualmente: "Dijo que estaba ocupado". Lo usas a diario en el trabajo: resumir juntas, reportar lo que dijo un cliente, pasar mensajes.

### Cómo se forma
**Regla general:** si el verbo introductorio está en pasado (*said, told*), el tiempo **retrocede un paso**.

| Estilo directo | Estilo indirecto |
|---|---|
| "I **work** remotely." | She said (that) she **worked** remotely. |
| "I'**m testing** it." | He said he **was testing** it. |
| "I **fixed** it." | He said he **had fixed** it. |
| "I'**ve finished**." | She said she **had finished**. |
| "I **will** call you." | He said he **would** call me. |
| "I **can** help." | She said she **could** help. |
| "You **must** update it." | He said I **had to** update it. |

**Cambian también referencias de tiempo y lugar:** today → that day · tomorrow → the next day · yesterday → the day before · here → there · this → that · now → then.

**Say vs. tell:**
- **say** (something): She **said** (that) she was tired.
- **tell** + persona: She **told me** (that) she was tired. ~~She said me~~ ✗

**Preguntas:** orden de afirmación, sin *do/does/did*:
- "Where **do you live**?" → He asked me where **I lived**.
- "**Are** you ready?" → She asked **if/whether** I **was** ready.

**Órdenes y peticiones:** *tell/ask + persona + to + verbo*
- "Send the report." → He told me **to send** the report.
- "Please don't push to main." → She asked us **not to push** to main.

**Cuándo no retroceder:** si sigue siendo cierto o el verbo está en presente: *He says he works remotely.* / *She said the API is down* (y sigue caída).

### Otros verbos útiles
explain, mention, suggest, promise, warn, admit, deny, confirm, agree.
- *The client **confirmed** that the budget **had been approved**.*

### Errores comunes de hispanohablantes
- ~~He said me…~~ → He **told** me… / He said **to** me…
- ~~She asked me where did I live.~~ → …where **I lived**.
- ~~He told me that call him.~~ → He told me **to call** him.

## Autoevaluación

> [!question]- Pasa a indirecto: "We will deliver the feature tomorrow," the PM said.
> The PM said (that) they **would deliver** the feature **the next day**.

> [!question]- Pasa a indirecto: "Can you review my PR?" she asked me.
> She asked me **if I could review** her PR.

> [!question]- Corrige: "He said me that I must to update the docs."
> He **told me** (that) I **had to** update the docs.

- Resume en estilo indirecto (6 frases) lo que se dijo en tu última junta de equipo.
