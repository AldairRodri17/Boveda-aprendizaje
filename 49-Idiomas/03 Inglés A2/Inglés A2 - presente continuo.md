# Inglés A2 - presente continuo

Parte de: [[49-Idiomas/03 Inglés A2/_Índice Inglés A2|Inglés A2]]

> [!info] Cómo avanzar
> `progreso`: **pendiente** → **viendo** → **visto** → **dominado**.

## Contenido

### Qué es
El **presente continuo** (o progresivo) describe acciones **en curso**: ahora mismo, en esta temporada, o planes futuros ya arreglados. Equivale a "estoy trabajando".

### Cómo se forma
**Sujeto + am/is/are + verbo-ing**
| | Ejemplo |
|---|---|
| Afirmativa | I'**m working** on the API. |
| Negativa | She **isn't coming** today. |
| Pregunta | **Are** you **using** Docker? |

**Reglas del -ing:**
- Normal: work → work**ing**
- Termina en -e: write → writ**ing**, make → mak**ing**
- Consonante-vocal-consonante acentuada: run → run**ning**, stop → stop**ping**
- -ie → -y: lie → l**ying**

### Usos
1. **Ahora mismo:** I'm reviewing your PR right now.
2. **Temporal / esta temporada:** We're migrating to Azure this quarter.
3. **Futuro planeado:** I'm meeting the client on Friday.
4. **Cambios en progreso:** AI is changing the industry.

### Presente simple vs. continuo
| Presente simple | Presente continuo |
|---|---|
| Hábitos: I **work** from home on Mondays. | Ahora: I'**m working** from the office today. |
| Hechos: Water **boils** at 100 °C. | Temporal: She'**s living** with her parents for now. |

**Verbos de estado** que normalmente **no** van en continuo: know, like, love, want, need, believe, understand, belong, seem.
- ~~I'm knowing the answer.~~ → I **know** the answer.
- ~~I'm wanting a coffee.~~ → I **want** a coffee.

### Errores comunes de hispanohablantes
- Omitir *to be*: ~~I working.~~ → I**'m** working.
- Usar continuo para hábitos: ~~I'm going to the gym every day.~~ → I **go** to the gym every day.
- ~~Writting, comming~~ → **writing, coming**.

## Autoevaluación

> [!question]- ¿Simple o continuo?: "Normally I (drive) to work, but today I (take) the metro."
> Normally I **drive**, but today I'**m taking** the metro.

> [!question]- Corrige: "I am needing more time."
> I **need** more time (need es verbo de estado).

> [!question]- Escribe en -ing: run, write, study.
> running, writing, studying.
